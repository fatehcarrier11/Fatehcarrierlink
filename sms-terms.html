1. Program Description

Fateh Carrier INC uses SMS for business-related conversations with freight brokers and other business contacts. Messages may relate to load inquiries, load coordination, pickup and delivery appointments, shipment status updates, and transportation-related documentation.

2. Consent and How Conversations Begin

When a broker or business contact initiates a text conversation with Fateh Carrier INC, we may respond to the inquiry and send relevant follow-up messages related to that business conversation. Where consent is required for a message or messaging program, we will obtain it before sending those messages.
