2. Consent and How Conversations Begin

Fateh Carrier INC obtains explicit SMS consent from freight brokers and business contacts by email before sending text messages. Contacts must confirm that they agree to receive business-related SMS messages at their designated mobile number. We retain records of consent.

Messages may relate to load inquiries, rate confirmations, load coordination, pickup and delivery appointments, shipment updates, and transportation-related documentation. A publicly listed phone number or an email address alone does not constitute consent to receive SMS messages.
