2. Consent and How Conversations Begin

Fateh Carrier Link obtains permission from freight brokers and business contacts by email before sending SMS messages. We ask contacts to explicitly confirm that they agree to receive business-related text messages at their designated mobile number.

We retain records of SMS consent. Messages may relate to load inquiries, load coordination, rate confirmations, pickup and delivery appointments, shipment updates, and transportation-related documentation.

A publicly listed phone number or email address alone does not constitute consent to receive SMS messages. We send SMS messages only after the recipient has provided consent.
