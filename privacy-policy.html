Privacy Policy

Fateh Carrier INC

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Fateh Carrier INC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and business information you provide to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with us:

  • Name, company name, and business contact details.
  • Business email addresses and mobile or telephone numbers.
  • Information needed for freight loads, rate confirmations, bookings, pickups, deliveries, and shipment updates.
  • Messages, SMS consent records, and opt-out preferences.
  • Other information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as needed to:

  • Coordinate freight transportation and trucking operations.
  • Communicate with brokers, customers, carriers, and business partners.
  • Send requested business-related messages and shipment updates.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Maintain consent and opt-out records.
  • Meet applicable legal and business requirements.

3. SMS Text Messaging and Mobile Information

With the required consent, Fateh Carrier INC may send business-related SMS messages about freight loads, rate confirmations, bookings, pickup and delivery appointments, shipment updates, and related trucking operations.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for assistance.

SMS consent, text messaging opt-in data, and mobile phone numbers will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers to operate our business messaging services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide those services on our behalf and may not use SMS consent information or mobile phone numbers for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant business information with service providers or business partners when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, coordinate shipments, comply with the law, protect our rights, or meet legal obligations.

Any such sharing is subject to applicable requirements. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent or opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, consent records, dispute resolution, and legal requirements.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may process basic technical information needed to operate and maintain the site. If analytics or tracking tools are used, they may collect information such as browser type, device details, or pages visited, according to the tools' settings and policies.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about your information or request correction or deletion where applicable law permits. You can stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP or contact us below for assistance.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. Please review their privacy policies separately.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business contacts and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through this website.
