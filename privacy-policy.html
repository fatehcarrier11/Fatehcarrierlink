Privacy Policy

Fateh Carrier INC
Effective date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Fateh Carrier INC collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or contact us about trucking, freight transportation, and related business services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • Name, company name, and business contact information.
  • Phone numbers, email addresses, and messages you send us.
  • Freight, load, pickup, delivery, and shipment details.
  • Information needed to respond to inquiries and provide services.
  • Information needed for business records, security, and legal compliance.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, coordinate loads and transportation, provide relevant pickup and delivery updates, exchange necessary documents, manage business relationships, comply with legal obligations, and protect our business and systems.

4. SMS and Mobile Information

If you communicate with Fateh Carrier INC by SMS, we may use your phone number and message content to respond to your inquiry and coordinate transportation-related business. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers necessary to deliver the messaging service.

5. Information Sharing

We may share relevant information with service providers that support our operations, including communications and technology providers. We may also disclose information when necessary to provide transportation services, comply with the law, respond to lawful requests, prevent fraud, or protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information. SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

6. Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for business, operational, recordkeeping, and legal purposes.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us with questions about your information. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP. For SMS help, reply HELP.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
